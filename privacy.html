Today's Play — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Today's Play shows live music events near a place you choose and plays short previews of the artists' songs. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. The app's developer does not run a server and does not collect, store or sell any personal information.

Location

If you tap “Who's playing near me”, the app asks your device for your approximate location. It is used only to search for nearby events: the coordinates are converted to a short location code and sent to the Ticketmaster Discovery API with that search. Your location is not saved and is not sent anywhere else. You can decline location access and search by city or ZIP code instead.

Searches

When you search by city or ZIP code, the place name or ZIP code is sent to a free lookup service (Open-Meteo for cities, Zippopotam.us for ZIP codes) to find its coordinates, and then to Ticketmaster to find events.

Music previews

To find songs, the names of the bands in your results are sent to the Deezer API and, if Deezer has no match, to the Apple iTunes Search API. Previews are streamed from those services.

Third-party services

These services receive your device's IP address and the requests described above, as with any website, and handle them under their own privacy policies:

  • Ticketmaster — event listings and the ticket pages the app links to
  • Deezer — top songs and previews
  • Apple (iTunes Search API) — fallback previews
  • Open-Meteo — city lookup
  • Zippopotam.us — ZIP code lookup

Data stored on your device

The app saves the last place you searched in your browser's local storage so it's filled in next time, and keeps a copy of its own files so it can open without a connection. Nothing else is stored. Clearing the app's storage removes it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
